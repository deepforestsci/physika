Quantum Mechanics
=================

In this tutotial we will introducing **Quantum Mechanics**. It is the branch of
physics that describes how matter and energy behave at the scale of atoms,
electrons and molecules. **Classical Mechanics** works well in describing everyday
objects, but it cannot correctly explain many microscopic events. **Quantum 
mechanics** provides a framework for explaining them.

We will be mainly focusing on **Time Independent Schrodinger Equation**. We use
this form when the events in the system do not evolve over time or when we want
to focus on only Spatial transformation of the system. This approach also allows
us to simplify the equation for quick and easy learning of **Quantum Mechanics**.

Wave Function
-------------

**Wave function** is a mathematical profile of the quantum state of a particle.

It is represented as:

.. math::

   \psi(x)


For this tutorial we will be using exponential of negative square of x. Where x
is the position of the particle in one dimention.

.. code-block::

    def psi(x: ℝ): ℝ:
        return exp(-x**2)
